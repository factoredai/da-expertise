To foster deep knowledge and innovation, the CoE is organized into specialized Expertise Groups. These groups serve as internal hubs for developing best practices and training talent. 

### 1. Analytics Engineering & Platform
* **Focus:** Turning individual engineering excellence into standardized, scalable data foundations.
* **Key Tech & Concepts:** Data modeling, pipeline orchestration, and enterprise observability via `dbt`, `Airflow`, and `Snowflake`.
* **Directory:** `/analytics_engineering_platform`

### 2. Growth & Optimization Analytics
* **Focus:** Defining and scaling the "Growth Analytics" methodology, turning experimentation into measurable business impact.
* **Key Tech & Concepts:** Causal inference, experimental design, funnel analysis, and segmentation via `Python`, `R`, and `Amplitude`.
* **Directory:** `/growth_optimization_analytics`

### 3. Strategic & Executive Analytics
* **Focus:** Translating data into high-level strategic decisions, mastering data narrative, and linking insights directly to financial impact and shareholder value.
* **Key Tech & Concepts:** Financial literacy, business strategy acumen, and automated executive reporting systems.
* **Directory:** `/strategic_executive_analytics`

## 🛠️ Repository Governance & Contributions

To maintain high code quality and secure environments, please follow these strict guidelines:

### Contribution Process
1. **Branching:** Never push directly to `main`. Create a feature branch using the prefix of your group (e.g., `growth/feature-ab-testing-template`).
2. **Pull Requests:** All PRs require at least **one approved review** from a designated code owner before merging.
3. **Documentation:** Any tool, macro, or notebook added must be documented in its respective folder's local `README.md`.

> ⚠️ **Security Warning:** Do not commit hardcoded credentials, API keys, or proprietary client data into this repository. Ensure your environment variables are stored locally and caught by the `.gitignore`.
